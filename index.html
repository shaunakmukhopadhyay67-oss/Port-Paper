What happens

  1. The form is scanned for labels with a blank beside or below them, such as Name of ship, IMO No. or Last port of call.
  2. Each label is matched to a row of your ship table. You check the matches and correct any that are wrong. Corrections are remembered for the next form.
  3. You download the same file with the blanks filled. Layout, logos and borders stay as the port sent them.

Scanned PDFs (photos of paper) and old .doc / .xls files cannot be read. Open old files in Word or Excel and save them as .docx / .xlsx first.
